# Art direction: Storybook Medical (channel format)

- **Look:** hand-painted 2.5D storybook illustration; ink outlines, gouache grain, amber key light, teal shadows (styles/storybook-medical.yaml).
- **Motion:** every still moves (slow push, slide, tilt); AI clips only for single actions; hard cuts every 1.1–2.7 s; whip for flashbacks, white flash for the gunshot and the time jump.
- **Type:** Montserrat 900, uppercase, thick charcoal stroke. Captions 3 words per page, lower-middle; active word amber, punch words red and larger.
- **Signature devices:** slammed date stamps (1825 / 1880) with an oxblood stroke, amber-ruled location and name cards, the rolling experiment counter, ✗/✓ verdict chips, the depth gauge that drops to 8 FT, and the pocket-watch snap that loops back to the hook.
