## Diagnosis
1. **The first second is the weak point everywhere.** S1 stayed-to-watch 31.6%, S3 55.2%, both under the ~60% line;
   Facebook avg watch 6-10 s; TikTok S2 25% of the video. S3's opening (a paradox: "the most famous stomach ... a man
   who kept walking away") held almost twice as many as S1's.
2. **Instagram is the surprise.** S3 reached 1,969 views in 1 day, more than all YouTube Shorts together. One post, so
   not a rule yet.
3. **TikTok is flat:** each post stops at 640-700 views (the first test pool). None broke out.
4. **The long video gets no traffic from the Shorts.** 772 YouTube Short views -> ~0 long views. CTR 3.3% is below
   the ~4% line but on 61 impressions it means nothing yet.
