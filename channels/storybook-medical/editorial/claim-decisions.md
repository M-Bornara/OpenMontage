# Claim decisions — Season Zero (final wording)

Decided 2026-10-18. Every item where sources differed, or where the evidence was thin, is resolved here.
Scripts must use the **wording** column.

## How we decide
1. **Closest to the event wins.** Order of trust:
   - the person who was there and wrote it down at the time (Beaumont, Harlow, the Lancet case team);
   - a peer-reviewed or scholarly study of those records (Macmillan, Waller);
   - later chronicles and accounts;
   - popular retellings.
2. **Two good sources that agree** beat one detailed source.
3. **If no source settles it,** pick the most probable version and **soften the wording** so it stays true
   either way ("about", "within days", "one chronicle says").
4. **If it matters but can't be known,** say so on screen ("nobody knows", "the records don't say"). For
   this channel, that is often the best line in the video.
5. **Never fill a gap with an invented detail.** Leave it out instead.
