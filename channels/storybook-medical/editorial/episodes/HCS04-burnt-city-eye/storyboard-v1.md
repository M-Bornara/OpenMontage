# HCS04 — The 5,000-Year-Old Golden Eye · storyboard v1 (for approval)

Rules: `plan/production-v3.md` (decoupage L28, Matt cameo L29). Script: `research.md` script v1 (approved 2026-11-06).
Format: 9:16 Short, 1080×1920. Voice: Liam, the 2026-11-04 setup. No end sting (Shorts loop).
**Length:** 116 words at the HCS02 pace (2.39 words/s with pauses) ≈ **48.5 s** (target 40–50).
**Care:** an ancient woman, no likeness exists and none is taken from any modern reconstruction: an illustrated
stand-in. Her remains are never shown as a skeleton: the grave is closed by mourners, the bone appears only as a
small surface under a microscope. The 2006 archaeologists are generic illustrated figures, not the real team.

**Fact check done (2026-11-08):** the eye was in her **left** socket (Moghadasi, *Iranian Journal of Public Health*
2014, PMC4449516; PopSci agrees). So she wears it on her left, in shots 5 and 9.
