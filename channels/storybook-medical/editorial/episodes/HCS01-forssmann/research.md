## Script v1 — He Pushed a Tube Into His Own Heart
`[excited]` In 1929, a young doctor pushed a tube through his own arm… all the way into his own heart.
Werner Forssmann was a trainee surgeon in a small German hospital. His idea: reach a living heart through a vein.
His boss said no. Far too dangerous.
So he needed help. The nurse in charge of the supplies agreed… as long as he tried it on HER.
`[mischievously]` He tricked her. He numbed his own arm instead.
Then he slid a thin catheter into a vein. Sixty-five centimeters.
And with the tube still in his arm, he walked to the X-ray room.
`[curious]` The picture showed the tip… inside his heart. He was fine.
His reward? He was fired from his next job.
Twenty-seven years later, the world caught up. Nobel Prize.
`[serious]` His later life was darker: he joined the Nazi Party in 1932.
