## Script v1 — She Was Trapped Under Ice for 80 Minutes
`[curious]` Northern Norway, 1999. A young doctor skiing with friends went head first… through the ice of a frozen river.
Anna Bågenholm was trapped. Above the ice, her friends could see only her skis and legs.
`[excited]` Underneath, she found a pocket of air… and kept breathing.
Her friends fought to get her out. After about forty minutes, her heart stopped.
They cut her free after eighty minutes.
No pulse. No breathing. Her body temperature: thirteen point seven degrees Celsius.
`[whispers]` Anywhere else… that's dead.
But at the hospital in Tromsø, doctors follow an old rule: nobody is dead… until they're warm and dead.
So they kept up CPR, and warmed her blood with a heart-lung machine.
At 10:15 that night… her heart started beating again.
`[curious]` Today, she works as a doctor.
