## Spine
- **Who:** Max von Pettenkofer, 73, Munich's famous hygiene professor (C02, C38).
- **Wants:** to prove that the cholera germ alone can't cause an epidemic (C04).
- **Against:** Robert Koch's germ theory, right after Hamburg lost about 8,600 people (C05, C06).
- **Stakes:** his life, and the theory he built his life on.
- **The choice:** his students offered to take the risk for him; he refused and drank it himself (C18).
- **The cost:** two days later, a week of cramps, his gut swarming with living cholera germs (C19, C20).
- **The payoff:** he declared victory; his critics said he'd simply had a mild case; and the baking soda he took first made the germs *more* likely to take hold (C25, C26, C33).
