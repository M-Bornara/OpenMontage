# HC001 — Alexis St. Martin: The Man With a Window Into His Stomach

Family: Extraordinary Case (+ Medical History)

**Verdict: APPROVE for research → script.** The person is strong and the evidence base is strong:
Beaumont's own 1833 book is a public-domain primary source. The opening can be illustrated cleanly, and
there is a natural myth-vs-record chapter.

**The two Shorts already made are REWRITE.** The pilot "Window into the Stomach" and ep 2 "Human Test Tube"
predate the v3 standard and carry unverified or overstated claims (`claims.csv` C06–C09, C14). Their art is
reusable; their narration and packaging are not.

## Editorial thesis
- **Worth telling:** an accident turned a fur-trade worker into the source of the first systematic
  observations of digestion inside a living person.
- **What we add:**
  - the human relationship and power imbalance that most retellings skip;
  - a clear line between what Beaumont recorded and what later legend added (the tasting, the burial, "238").

## Risks
- **Tier A claims still open:** date, weapon and distance; experiment count; "tasted"; the burial. All are
  UNKNOWN until checked.
- **Medical level 1** (basic digestion physiology): needs one authoritative physiology source.
- **Advertiser safety:** the opening is always shown as a clean illustrated port, never a wound.
- **People-first:** St. Martin is the protagonist, not "the stomach".
