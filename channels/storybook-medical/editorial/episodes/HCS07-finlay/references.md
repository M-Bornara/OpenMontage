## What the photos changed (storyboard v1.1)
- Finlay: bald crown, moustache joined to side whiskers, **shaved chin**, round wire spectacles (v1 wrongly said a full beard).
- Lazear: **full trimmed beard**, centre-parted hair (v1 said clean-shaven).
- Reed: moustache, dark-blue uniform with shoulder cords and brass buttons.
- Havana: lime-plastered walls in faded colours with iron grilles, not bright pink and blue.
- Academy hall: the old San Agustín convent (the Academy's seat from 1868, revistaccuba.sld.cu); **no ceiling fans** in 1881.
- The yellow quarantine flag was flown by ships, so it goes on a ship in the harbour.
