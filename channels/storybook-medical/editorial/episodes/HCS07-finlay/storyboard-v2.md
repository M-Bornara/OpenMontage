# HCS07 — Nobody Believed Him About the Mosquito · storyboard v2 (story v2 approved 2026-11-15)

Rules: `plan/production-v3.md` (decoupage L28, Matt cameo L29, pre-viz L36/L37), `plan/culture-themes.md`, the Shorts
safe zone (text only in y 288–1248). Facts: `research.md`. Format: 9:16 Short, 1080×1920, Liam. No end sting (loop).
Slot: **Thu Dec 3** = Finlay's birthday and Latin American Medicine Day (verified 2026-11-12).
**Care:** yellow fever shown as a ship under the yellow quarantine flag (a ship's flag, not a house's), shuttered
doors with mourning bows, an empty cot; never sick bodies. The mosquito is a sharp drawn silhouette, never a bite
close-up with blood.
**v1.1 (2026-11-14), from the period photos (`references.md`):** Finlay has a white moustache joined to side
whiskers, a shaved chin and round wire spectacles (not a full beard); Lazear has a full trimmed beard; the hall has
no ceiling fans (anachronistic in 1881: hand fans instead); Lazear's bite follows his own account (a stray mosquito
while feeding a lab mosquito on a patient), because "deliberate" is disputed.
**Theme (new, needs your OK):** `caribbean-colonial` · font Montserrat Black · music: Cuban habanera / contradanza,
slow, minor: solo piano, tres guitar, soft güiro and claves; curious, patient, warm. Western instruments are right
here (19th-century Havana), so no "no Western orchestra" line.

## Script v2 (approved 2026-11-15: "new scripts looks much better go for it")

```
[curious] He found what spreads yellow fever. Then spent nineteen years trying to make anyone believe him.
[serious] Havana, 1881. Yellow fever was killing thousands, and nobody knew how it spread.
[curious] Doctor Carlos Finlay named a tiny suspect… the mosquito.
He told the Academy of Sciences. [pause] Almost nobody believed him.
So he kept going. Breeding mosquitoes. Testing. Year after year.
Then, in 1900, a US Army team led by Walter Reed finally tested his idea… with Finlay's own mosquitoes.
[serious] One of them, Jesse Lazear, was bitten… [pause] and died of yellow fever.
The mosquito was the killer. Finlay had been right all along.
[curious] Reed got most of the fame. But Reed himself gave the credit… to Finlay.
```
