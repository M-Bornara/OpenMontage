## Spine
- **Who:** Carlos Finlay, a Havana doctor.
- **Wants:** to be believed that a mosquito spreads yellow fever.
- **Against:** the Academy's disbelief, and nineteen years.
- **Stakes:** an epidemic killing thousands while the answer sits ignored.
- **The choice:** he keeps breeding mosquitoes and testing, year after year.
- **The cost:** the proof costs a life: Jesse Lazear.
- **Payoff:** someone else gets the fame, and that man hands the credit back.
