# HCS05 — The Doctor Who Refused to Treat Half His Patients · research + script v1 (approved 2026-11-06)

**Thumbnail sentence:** 1,100 years ago, a doctor left some patients untreated on purpose, to see if his cure worked.
**Family:** Crazy Science. **Format:** Story Short. **Core rating:** DOCUMENTED (one passage in Kitab al-Hawi).
"The first clinical trial ever" is an OVERSTATEMENT and becomes the myth check.

## Script v1 (~45 s) · approved 2026-11-06 (moved to Mon Dec 7, two weeks after the golden eye)
```
[curious] Eleven hundred years ago, a doctor refused to treat some of his patients. On purpose.
Al-Razi was a Persian physician from Rey, near today's Tehran.
For the first signs of meningitis, the treatment was bloodletting.
Al-Razi wasn't sure it worked.
So he bled one group of patients… and deliberately left another group alone.
Then he watched what happened to both.
The bled group did better, he wrote, in his giant medical encyclopedia. So he kept the treatment.
[mischievously] He was wrong. Bloodletting doesn't help meningitis.
[curious] Some call it the first clinical trial. Not quite. But comparing two groups is how we test every medicine today.
```
