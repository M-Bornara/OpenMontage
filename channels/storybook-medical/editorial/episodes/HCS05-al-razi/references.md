## What the research changed (storyboard v1.1)
- Writing on **paper** (Baghdad paper from the late 700s), sitting on a carpet or cushion, sheet on the knee, reed pen
  and inkwell: no wooden manuscript stand, no desk (user's note).
- The *Hawi* was compiled after his death from his notebooks by his students (NLM "Islamic Medical Manuscripts";
  Encyclopedia.com), so shot 7a shows piles of notebooks and the stamp reads "HIS NOTES → KITAB AL-HAWI".
- Script wording to consider (not changed): "…he wrote, in his giant medical encyclopedia" could become "…he wrote,
  in the notes that became his giant encyclopedia". The approved line stays unless the user wants it.
