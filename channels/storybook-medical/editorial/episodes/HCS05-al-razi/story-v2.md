## Spine
- **Who:** al-Razi, physician of Rey, c. 900.
- **Wants:** to know if the standard cure for early meningitis (bloodletting) works.
- **Against:** the cure everyone uses; nobody tests cures.
- **Stakes:** feverish patients; withholding a cure, or giving a useless one.
- **The choice:** he bleeds one group and deliberately leaves the other alone (P1).
- **The turn:** his own test says the cure works… and it doesn't.
- **Payoff:** wrong answer, right method; the method outlived the answer.
