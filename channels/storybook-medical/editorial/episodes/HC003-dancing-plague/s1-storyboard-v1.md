## Script (approved 2026-11-21, fact gate 21 PASS: fact-gate/2026-11-21-script-s1-v3.md)

```
[intense] Strasbourg, 1518. A woman steps into the street… and starts to dance.
She can't stop.
[curious] No music. Her name, the chronicles say, was Frau Troffea.
She danced for hours… until she collapsed.
Next morning, she was up again. On swollen feet. Dancing.
[quiet] Her husband begged her to stop. She didn't.
[fast] By the third day, a crowd was watching.
[pause] Then… others started dancing too.
Within days, more than thirty.
Frightened, the city sent her away in a wagon… to a saint's shrine.
[pause] [whispers] But the dancing didn't leave with her.
[curious] The city's doctors had a cure: more dancing.
[excited] So the city built stages… and paid musicians to play.
One chronicle says that within a month, four hundred people had joined the dancing.
[quiet] And nobody knows why it started.
```
