## The channel in one paragraph
**Oddly Matt** tells true stories from medicine, history, science and survival as hand-painted, illustrated
cinematic films: 7/10 entertainment, documentary-level fact standards. Every story has a documented core; legends
are labelled (fact meter: DOCUMENTED · LIKELY · DISPUTED · LEGEND). Matt is the curious host character with amber
"?" glasses hidden in every film ("spot Matt's glasses" is the standing comment game). Narrator: **Liam** (ElevenLabs
AI voice), a fixed decision. Owner: Mahdi Moradi (Calgary). Started as "Storybook Medical" / "The Human Casebook" in
the Business Mastermind Council project (score 5.85, run as a capped Lab experiment).

- Tagline: "True stories from the strange side of being human."
- Short bio: "True stories too strange to sound true. Illustrated. Fact-checked."
- Palette: amber `#E8A33D`, teal `#1F4E5A`, bone `#EFE6D2`, charcoal `#22262A` · font Montserrat.
- Audience: English-speaking teens and adults (US, Canada, UK, Australia). **Not made for kids.**
- Never: medical advice, "healthier lives", gore thumbnails, caduceus or other medical clichés, child-oriented tone.

## Formats and cadence
- **Shorts** 35–55 s, complete mini-stories (never trailers). **Long videos** 5–10 min, every two weeks, Sunday.
- One YouTube upload every 2 days through Dec 13; TikTok/Instagram/Facebook copies the next day.
- Times: long Sun 11:00 ET (10:00 AB); Shorts 15:00 ET (14:00 AB); other platforms 18:00 ET (17:00 AB).
- Max 2 Shorts per long story, never back to back. Max one non-Western story per week.
- Ready-by rule: finished and privately uploaded 4 days before its slot. A missed slot beats a weak Short.
- Playlists (fixed set): Season Zero + story families (Strange Medical Cases, Crazy Science, History Got It Wrong,
  Weirdest Moments, Impossible Survival).
