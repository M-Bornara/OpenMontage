## 1. YouTube (do this on a computer)

Go to **studio.youtube.com**, then **Customization** in the left menu.

**Branding tab**
1. **Picture** → Upload → `profile-icon-800.png`. Keep the default crop (the circle shows the glasses) → Done.
2. **Banner image** → Upload → `youtube-banner-2560x1440.png`. The preview shows TV, desktop and phone crops; "ODDLY MATT" should be readable in all three. → Done.
3. **Video watermark** → Upload → `youtube-watermark-150.png`. Display time: **Entire video**.
4. Click **Publish** (top right).

**Basic info tab**
1. **Name:** `Oddly Matt`
2. **Handle:** `oddlymatt` (already yours)
3. **Description:** paste this:

```
Stories too strange to sound true, except they actually happened.

Oddly Matt tells extraordinary true stories from medicine, history, science and survival, in painted-style illustrated films. Every story is researched from original records, and we tell you when the sources disagree.

Spot Matt's amber glasses hidden in every film.

Illustrations are AI-assisted artistic reconstructions; research, scripting and editing are human-directed. Not medical advice.

Business: hello@oddlymatt.com
```

4. **Links** → Add link, three times:
   - Title `Website`, URL `https://oddlymatt.com`
   - Title `TikTok`, URL `https://www.tiktok.com/@oddlymatt`
   - Title `Instagram`, URL `https://www.instagram.com/oddlymatt.tv`
   - Leave "Links on banner" at **1** (the website).
5. **Contact info → Email:** `hello@oddlymatt.com`
6. Click **Publish**.

**Settings** (gear icon, bottom left)
1. **Channel → Basic info:** Country of residence = your country. Keywords:
   `true stories, medical history, history stories, strange history, animated history, science stories, survival stories, illustrated stories`
2. **Channel → Advanced settings:** "Do you want to set your channel as made for kids?" → **No, set this channel as not made for kids.**
3. **Upload defaults → Basic info:** paste the last two lines of the description (the AI line and the business email) so every upload has them.
4. Save.

Later, when there are uploads: Customization → Layout → add a Channel trailer and a "For returning subscribers" featured video.

## 2. TikTok (phone app)

Profile → **Edit profile**:
1. **Change photo** → `profile-icon-1080.png`
2. **Name:** `Oddly Matt`
3. **Username:** `oddlymatt` (already set)
4. **Bio** (80 characters max), paste:
   `True stories too strange to sound true. Illustrated. Fact-checked.`
5. **YouTube / Instagram / Website:** not offered on new accounts (checked 2026-10-18). TikTok shows them later,
   usually around 1,000 followers. Add YouTube, @oddlymatt.tv and `https://oddlymatt.com` when they appear.

Settings and privacy → **Account**: keep it a **Personal account**. Creator Rewards needs a personal account, and Business accounts only get the commercial music library.

## 3. Instagram @oddlymatt.tv (phone app)

1. Profile → **Edit profile**:
   - **Picture** → `profile-icon-1080.png`
   - **Name:** `Oddly Matt | True strange stories` (this field is searchable)
   - **Username:** `oddlymatt.tv`
   - **Bio**, paste:
     ```
     True stories from the strange side of being human.
     Illustrated. Fact-checked.
     Spot Matt's glasses in every film.
     ```
   - **Links** → Add external link: URL `https://oddlymatt.com`, title `Website`. Add a second: `https://www.youtube.com/@oddlymatt`, title `YouTube`.
2. Settings → **Account type and tools** → Switch to professional account → **Creator** → category **Video creator** (or "Digital creator"). Show category on profile: on.
3. Edit profile → **Contact options** → Email: `hello@oddlymatt.com`. Then Edit profile → **Profile display** →
   **Display contact info** on. Check it from a logged-out phone: an "Email" button shows under the bio (app only).
4. New accounts are often blocked from adding links for a few days; retry the links in step 1 after 2–3 days.

## 4. Facebook Page (computer)

Log in with your one real profile → menu → **Pages** → **Create new Page**:
1. **Page name:** `Oddly Matt`
2. **Category:** type `Video Creator` and select it (add `Education` as a second if offered)
3. **Bio:** `True stories from the strange side of being human. Illustrated, fact-checked short films.`
4. Create Page, then:
   - **Profile picture** → `profile-icon-1080.png`
   - **Cover photo** → `facebook-cover-1640x624.png`. Drag to position: the name should be centred. Save.
5. Page settings → **Page setup**:
   - **Username:** `oddlymatt` (or `oddlymatt.tv` if taken)
   - **Contact:** email `hello@oddlymatt.com`, website `https://oddlymatt.com`
6. **Add action button** → **Watch video** → link `https://www.youtube.com/@oddlymatt`.
7. In Meta Business Suite, link the Instagram account to the Page (Settings → Linked accounts) so you can post to both from one place.

---

## 5. Email

`hello@oddlymatt.com` forwards to your Gmail. Replies will come from the Gmail address unless you set up
"Send mail as" in Gmail (Settings → Accounts → Send mail as). That needs SMTP access from your email
provider. It's optional for now.

## 6. Website (later)

Until the one-page site exists, point oddlymatt.com to the YouTube channel (a domain forward/redirect at
your registrar), so the link in every bio works today.
