## 4. Posting rules

**Same video file everywhere** (the watermark-free `short.mp4`). Never re-upload a file downloaded from another app.

**Timing (unchanged for now):** YouTube first, the others the next day at 18:00 ET (17:00 Alberta in winter).
**Test after C001's 4 posts** (HCS02–HCS09 TikToks are already scheduled next day, 2026-11-11): post TikTok on the same day as YouTube for 4 Shorts and compare the 48-h views with the
next-day posts. Same-day wins → it becomes the rule.

**Scheduling:**
- YouTube: Studio, as in upload-schedule.md.
- TikTok: TikTok Studio → Upload → Schedule (up to 10 days ahead). Wait for the content check before scheduling.
- Instagram + Facebook: **Meta Business Suite → Planner**, both in one go. This replaces posting Instagram by hand
  (the open-items reminders for 17:00 Alberta are no longer needed).

**Captions: one per platform, not one for all.** Each `publish.md` gets three caption blocks that differ:

| Platform | First line | Body | Hashtags | Call to action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | the hook in words people search ("doctor drank bacteria", "stomach experiment 1825") | 1 short line | 3–5: one broad (#history), two topical (#medicalhistory, #archaeology), #oddlymatt | "Full story: YouTube, Oddly Matt" |
| Instagram | the hook, under 125 characters (that's what shows before "more") | 1–2 lines + the source name | 3–5, same idea | "Full story on YouTube (link in bio)" |
| Facebook | a plain-language opener that works without sound ("In 1825, a doctor fed a man through a hole in his stomach.") | 2–3 lines, a little more context | 1–3 | "Full story on YouTube: oddlymatt.com" |

Rules for every caption: the fact in the hook must be in the claims log; no medical advice; no "like if…" bait.

**Facebook opening test (cluster 2):** for 4 Shorts, add a 2–3 s on-screen text line in the first frame of the
Facebook copy only (the same claim as the hook). Compare average watch with the 4 before. If it moves past ~15 s,
keep it.

**Pinned or first comment on every platform:** the comment challenge from publish.md (comment-challenges.md), plus
on TikTok/Instagram/Facebook one line pointing to the long video when the Short belongs to a long story.

## 5. Bring people home

Right now no TikTok, Instagram or Facebook post tells anyone the long video exists.

1. **The call to action in every caption** (§4). It costs nothing.
2. **oddlymatt.com becomes a link page** instead of a redirect: Matt's glasses, the tagline, four buttons (YouTube,
   TikTok, Instagram, Facebook), the latest long video, and an email signup ("one true story a week"). One static
   page; it can be built in the Bornara stack at no cost. Every bio already points to oddlymatt.com, so nothing
   else changes.
3. **TikTok bio link:** a clickable link needs 1,000 followers on a personal account (a business account can get it
   sooner, but loses Creator Rewards). Until then, put "oddlymatt.com" in the bio as plain text
   ([Buffer, Dec 2025](https://buffer.com/resources/how-to-put-a-link-in-tiktok-bio)).
4. **Facebook:** invite your own friends to follow the Page once (Page → Invite friends). Only once, no spam.
5. **Newsletter:** start collecting emails on the link page from day one. The first issue goes out at 100 signups.
   It is the only audience no platform can take away.

- If Facebook stays under ~8 s average after the opening test, it stays a free copy and gets no extra effort.

## 10. Change tracking (user, 2026-11-11)
Every change to how we post (captions, comments, times, openings, settings, end screens) gets a row in
[`change-log.csv`](change-log.csv) before it goes live: what changed, the metric that judges it, the baseline, and
when we compare. Rules:
- **One change per platform at a time.** Two changes on the same posts can't be told apart.
- **At least 4 posts per side** before a verdict. One Short is noise at our view counts.
- **Keep** it if the main metric is better on at least 3 of the 4 posts and nothing else drops clearly; **revert**
  otherwise and note the result. (Starting rule: a guess, revisited at the day-90 review.)
- The weekly review fills the `result` and `decision` columns when a change reaches its compare point.
