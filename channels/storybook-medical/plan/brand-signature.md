# Oddly Matt — brand signature system (proposal, 2026-10-18)

The idea: viewers should recognise an Oddly Matt film in five seconds, with **no logo inside the story**. The
signatures live *in* the world, the way Hitchcock appeared in his own films.

## The core symbol: Matt's spectacles
- Round wire-rimmed spectacles, a design that exists in every era we cover (from the 1500s to today).
- **Our unique detail:** **amber-tinted lenses**, with a small **"?" glint** in the left lens.
  - Nobody else's glasses look like this, so it's ownable.
  - It's simple enough to work as a 32-pixel profile picture (two circles + a "?").
- One symbol, used three ways:

| Use | How |
|---|---|
| **Hidden object** | In every film the spectacles are left somewhere in the scene: on a shelf, a cart seat, a hospital tray, a snowy rock. This is the "spot it" game in the comments. |
| **Logo** | The spectacles icon = profile picture. "Oddly Matt" wordmark + spectacles = banner, end screen, thumbnails (small corner mark). |
| **Sting** | A 1-second intro or outro: the spectacles land on a book, and the "?" glints. Used at the start of long videos and on the end screen, never inside Shorts' first seconds. |

## The cameo: Matt, the time-travelling onlooker
- A **recurring background character**: the narrator's avatar, quietly present at every story like a
  witness. Examples:
  - a voyageur at the trading post;
  - a passenger on Gage's coach;
  - a face in the Strasbourg crowd;
  - a visitor in a 1929 hospital corridor;
  - a hiker on the ridge in Norway.
- **Wardrobe:** dressed for each era (so he never breaks history), but with **three constant signatures**:
  1. the amber spectacles;
  2. a **teal scarf** (a scarf exists in every era; teal is our house colour);
  3. a small **amber notebook** he's always writing in (the storyteller taking notes).
- **Rules:**
  - Exactly **one cameo per film**, in the background.
  - He never touches the story, never speaks, never stands where a fact happens, and isn't mentioned in
    narration.
  - He's an artistic signature, not a historical person. The description says so once in the channel's
    About page ("Spot Matt in every story").
- **Built once:** a character sheet (front, ¾, profile + 3 era outfits), ≈ $0.30 one-time.

## What we deliberately don't do
- **No "Oddly Matt" text painted into historical scenes** (signs, papers, posters). It breaks immersion and
  history, and feels like an ad.
- **No more than two signatures per film** (the spectacles + the cameo). Adding a signature bucket, hat
  and so on as well would turn the scenes into a mascot hunt and pull attention from the story.
  - If a third is ever wanted, the cameo's notebook already plays that role.
- **No burned-in watermark on Shorts** (it hurts reposting on other platforms). Long videos can use
  YouTube's own branding watermark (the spectacles icon, bottom-right).

## Open choices for the owner
1. **Cameo look:**
   - **Loosely based on you:** a stylised illustration, not a portrait; the persona becomes literally you.
   - **An original character.**
2. **Should the "?" glint and amber lenses become the logo?** (Final logo design is in the branding phase.)
3. **Where to announce the game:** in the About page only (subtle), or occasionally in the pinned comment
   ("Did you spot Matt's glasses?").
