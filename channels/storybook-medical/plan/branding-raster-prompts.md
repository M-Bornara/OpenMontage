# Oddly Matt: prompts for the painted brand images (Gemini / Flow)

Use **Gemini app → Create image (Nano Banana Pro / "Thinking" image model)**. Flow also works with
"Ingredients", but Gemini follows long prompts and reference images better.

**Attach these two images to every prompt** (drag them into the chat first):
1. `channels/storybook-medical/refs/style-anchor.jpg`: the style reference
2. `projects/oddly-matt-mascot/assets/images/refs/matt-a-witness.png`: Matt's character sheet

Tips:
- Start a **new chat for each image** so earlier images don't leak in.
- If it adds text or letters anywhere, reply: "Same image, remove all text and letters."
- To fix one thing, reply with only that change ("same image, make the sky darker"). Don't rewrite the prompt.
- Send me the ones you like. I'll re-run the winners through the Google API at **4K** (the app gives
  lower resolution, and its images may carry a visible Gemini mark), then place them on the canvas.

```

Image 2 is the character sheet of MATT: keep him exactly as drawn (long charcoal wool coat, wide-brimmed dark felt hat, round thin wire spectacles with amber-tinted lenses, teal knitted scarf with fringed ends, small amber leather notebook and pencil, calm small smile).

Paint a wide 16:9 cinematic storybook illustration, the key art for a channel of strange true stories from history and medicine.

COMPOSITION: Matt stands in the LEFT third, full body, three-quarter view, writing in his amber notebook and glancing toward the centre with a small knowing smile, his scarf tail lifting in a breeze. The CENTRE of the image is calm, open and darker (dusk sky and haze) so a title can sit there later; nothing important in the middle band. Behind and around him, one continuous dusk landscape blends several story worlds from left to right, small and far away, softened by atmospheric haze:
- a 1820s log fur-trading post with a wooden palisade on a lake shore, a canoe at the dock;
- an 1840s railroad cutting through green Vermont hills, a small steam locomotive with a plume of steam;
- the gothic cathedral spire of a 1500s European town with steep roofs, lit windows;
- a 1920s brick hospital building with rows of warm lit windows;
- far right, snowy Norwegian mountains and a frozen waterfall under the first stars.
Layers clearly separated: foreground (Matt, grassy rise), midground (buildings), background (mountains, sky) for parallax.

LIGHT AND COLOUR: golden hour fading to dusk. Warm amber key light (#E8A33D) from the upper left falling on Matt, deep teal (#1F4E5A) shadows and sky, bone-white (#EFE6D2) highlights, charcoal (#22262A) darkest tones, a touch of oxblood red (#7A2E2A) only in small details. Mysterious but warm and inviting, never dark or scary.

No text, no letters, no logos, no signs with writing, no watermark. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not glossy.
```
