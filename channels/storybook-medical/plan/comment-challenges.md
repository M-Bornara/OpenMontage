# Comment challenges

**Goal:** give viewers one fun, genuine reason to comment on every video, plus data we can use to choose the
next stories.

## The two layers
1. **The standing game (every video): "Spot Matt's glasses."** Viewers comment the timestamp where they found
   the amber spectacles. It costs nothing and rewards rewatching (which also helps retention). It's
   explained once in the About page and occasionally in a pinned comment.
2. **One story challenge per video**, chosen from the formats below. It's always tied to *this* story.

## Challenge formats (rotate them)
| Format | Example | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Would you?** | "Alexis refused the surgery. Would you have?" | Instant personal opinion, low effort to answer |
| **Pick a side** | "Brave genius or reckless? 🫀 or ⚠️" | One-tap answer with an emoji vote |
| **Your theory** | "Why did she start dancing? Historians still disagree." | A real open question, so the debate is genuine |
| **Top this** | "'Here is business enough for you.' Write a better understatement." | Creative and funny, the best ones get pinned |
| **Nominate** | "Worst medical cure in history? Nominate one." | Feeds our topic list |
| **Your story** | "Coldest you've ever been?" | Personal stories build community |
| **Next case** | "Next: Bågenholm or Koepcke?" (community poll) | Lets the audience choose the direction (Season Zero logic) |

## Where the challenge appears
- **Shorts:** **never in the narration** (it would break the story). It goes in:
  - the **pinned comment**, posted by us right after upload;
  - optionally, a 1-second **end-card line** over the final held frame (small, same caption style).
- **Long videos:** one natural spoken question in the last 30 seconds ("So… would you have signed?"), plus
  the pinned comment and a community-tab poll a few days later.

## Rules
- **No fake contests and no "comment X to win".** Giveaways are only allowed under YouTube's contest
  policy with written rules. We don't run any during the probe.
- **No "like if… / comment if…" bait.** The question must be worth answering on its own.
- **No planted errors** ("spot the mistake" is off-limits: accuracy is the brand).
- **Respond in the first hour.** Heart and reply to early comments. Pin the best answer to the challenge
  after 24 h.
- **Questions never trivialise suffering** ("Would you have survived?" is fine; jokes about victims are
  not).
- **Track it:** comments per 1,000 views, by format, in `analytics-review.md`. Keep the formats that work.
