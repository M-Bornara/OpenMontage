# Comment challenges

**Goal:** give viewers one fun, genuine reason to comment on every video, plus data we can use to choose the
next stories. The challenge is **chosen by score, never at random.**

## The two layers
1. **The standing game (every video): "Spot Matt's glasses."** Viewers comment the timestamp where they found
   the amber spectacles. It rewards rewatching, which also helps retention. It's explained once in the About
   page and occasionally in a pinned comment.
2. **One story challenge per video**, selected with the scoring below.

## Formats
| Format | Example | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| **Would you?** | "Alexis refused the surgery. Would you have?" | personal, low effort |
| **Pick a side** | "Who was right: Alexis 🛶 or Beaumont 🩺?" | one-tap emoji vote |
| **Your theory** | "Why did she start dancing? Historians still disagree." | genuine debate |
| **Top this** | "Write a better understatement." | creative, pin-worthy |
| **Nominate** | "Worst cure in history? Nominate one." | feeds our topic list |
| **Your story** | "What's the coldest you've ever been?" | community |
| **Did you know?** | "Did you know this rule? ✅ / ❌" | measures surprise |

## Scoring (1–5 per criterion)
| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **Story fit** | 30% | Does it come straight from the video's central moment or its WOW? |
| **Ease** | 20% | Can someone answer in one tap or one line? |
| **Debate** | 20% | Do answers genuinely differ (no single right answer)? |
| **Emotional pull** | 15% | Does the viewer feel personally involved? |
| **Data value** | 15% | Does the answer teach us something (tastes, myths, topics)? |

**Safety gate (pass/fail, before scoring).** A candidate fails if it:
- invites imitation of dangerous acts, or medical advice;
- asks people to share their own health conditions;
- trivialises a victim;
- is likely to turn political or toxic in a Short's comment section;
- is a "like if… / comment to win" bait.

**Modifiers and ties**
- **Variety:** −0.3 if the same format was used in either of the previous two uploads (release order).
- **No repeats:** the same question can't appear twice in a season, even in another story.
- **Tie-break:** data value, then variety.

**Where it appears**
- **Shorts:** the pinned comment (posted right after upload), plus an optional small line on the final held
  frame. **Never in the narration.**
- **Long videos:** one spoken question in the last 30 s, plus the pinned comment, plus a community poll a few
  days later.

**Rules**
- No giveaways during the probe (YouTube's contest policy requires formal rules).
- No planted errors.
- Reply and heart in the first hour; pin the best answer after 24 h.
- Track comments per 1,000 views by format in `analytics-review.md`, and adjust the weights after
  Season Zero.
