## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** The Doctor Who Drank Bacteria

**Description:**
```
1984, Perth, Australia. Barry Marshall and pathologist Robin Warren had found spiral bacteria living in inflamed stomachs. Most doctors blamed ulcers on stress, and almost nobody believed bacteria could cause them.

The bacteria wouldn't infect his lab pigs, so Marshall drank a culture himself. Within days he was vomiting. On day 10, a biopsy showed the bacteria and an inflamed stomach. His wife Adrienne suggested it was time to treat himself.

In 2005, Marshall and Warren won the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine. In the Nobel Assembly's words, peptic ulcer became "a disease that can be cured by a short regimen of antibiotics and acid secretion inhibitors." This is a history story, not medical advice.

More true medical stories: "The Man With a Window Into His Stomach" (linked above).
```
