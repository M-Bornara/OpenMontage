## YouTube Shorts
**Title:** She Was Trapped Under Ice for 80 Minutes

**Description:**
```
1999, near Narvik, Norway. Anna Bågenholm, a young doctor, was skiing with two colleagues when she fell head first through the ice of a frozen river. Under the ice she found a pocket of air and kept breathing, while her friends held on to her skis.

After about 40 minutes her heart stopped. Rescuers cut her free after 80 minutes and flew her to the University Hospital of North Norway in Tromsø. Her body temperature was 13.7 °C. The doctors followed an old rule: nobody is dead until they are warm and dead. They warmed her blood with a heart-lung machine, and at 10:15 that night her heart started beating again.

After about 9 hours of resuscitation and stabilisation she went on to a good recovery, and she works as a doctor today. This is a history story, not medical advice.

More true medical stories: "The Man With a Window Into His Stomach" (linked above).
```
